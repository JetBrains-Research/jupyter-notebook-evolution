# JetBrains Research Hackathon 2023: Information and Regulations

# Introduction 

Hello everyone\! First of all, thank you for participating in our hackathon\! This event is organized in partnership with ETH Zurich, and its main goal is to provide you with an opportunity to show your skills in data analysis and machine learning. From our side, we are interested in how you work inside Jupyter notebooks, since they are an actively developing medium. During the course of this hackathon, we will be collecting the data on how you code in the setting of a Jupyter notebook, in what order you write, how you split the code by cells, etc. For this reason, during the competition, we will ask you to use only the provided Jupyter Notebook environment for solving the tasks. Even though we cannot share more information about our study at this point in order not to bias your work, we can share the results with you later if you want. 

We hope that you will have fun solving these tasks\! 

# Schedule

The hackathon will be conducted on May 6th, starting at 10:00 CET and finishing at 23:59 CET on the same day. 

The schedule for May 6th:

* 10:00 CET — Introduction call.  
* 19:59 CET — Submission deadline.  
* 20:30 CET — Presentation of submissions (1.5 hours). 

The results will be published online before 23:59 CET, May 12th. There will be no appealing process, however, the organizers might contact participants for clarifications in case of a discrepancy in the submitted and replicated results.

# Process 

In this section, we will cover the organizational process of the hackathon. If you will have any additional questions, do not hesitate to contact the organizers: [Sergey Titov](mailto:Sergey.Titov@jetbrains.com)or [Konstantin Grotov](mailto:konstantin.grotov@jetbrains.com).

### Eligibility

One of the goals of the hackathon is collecting the data about the solving process, and because of that, we require participants to solve the tasks in a specific environment. In order for the solution to be eligible, the following requirements should be met:

- Participants successfully registered in the data collection tool that we provide.  
- Solutions for the tasks were written in the specific dedicated files and in the Web version of Jupyter Notebooks (without using the IDE).  
- Solution repository includes log files.  
- Final result can be reproduced.

The instructions for registering in the data collection tool and the specific structure of a submission will be shared a bit later.

### Tasks

The hackathon consists of two tasks:

- A data analysis task, where you need to wrangle data, implement metrics, and visualize results.  
- A machine learning task, where you need to beat the provided baseline.

Both tasks will be shared with you at the start of the hackathon. 

### Presentation

At the end of the hackathon you may present your work. This part is not obligatory, but it may affect your final score (see evaluation criteria below). The format is the following – you will have 2 minutes to present any interesting findings that you encountered during the course of the hackathon. This could be models that you tried and that worked better than expected, or it could be some beautiful plots from a data analysis task – anything worth noting. 

### Submission

All submissions are accepted in the form of a link to the GitHub repository. This repository should be a fork of the one that will be provided at the start of the hackathon. We will only consider solutions in the main branch with the latest commit before May 6th, 19:59 CET.

## Prizes and Evaluation Criteria

We provide 5 prizes:

1st place: 380$ Amazon gift card.  
2nd place: 300$ Amazon gift card.  
3rd place: 200$ Amazon gift card.  
4th and 5th places: 100$ Amazon gift card.

Each task will have its own leaderboard, and the overall hackathon placement will be decided as the average rank between leaderboards. We use the following criteria for the tasks:

- The data analysis task will be scored by a team of experts, the evaluation criteria will be provided with the task. The leaderboard will be constructed based on the mean score between experts.  
- The machine learning task leaderboard will be constructed based on a metric that will be provided with the task.

In case of the same final rank, the priority will be given to one with the best individual rank among both tasks. If participants have the same best ranks, the priority will be given to the one with the best presentation (will be judged by experts separately in case of the draw).
